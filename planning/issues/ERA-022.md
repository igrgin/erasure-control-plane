# ERA-022: Manage retention holds, blockers, and source-owner intervention

Epic: [EPIC-06](../epics/EPIC-06.md). Milestone: v0.1 end-to-end demonstration. Status: proposed.

Delivery and acceptance owner: project owner. Source-behavior decisions also require the accountable source owner.

## Outcome

An operator can route blocked work to the accountable owner and retain the reason for its resolution.

## Requirements and dependencies

Requirements: REQ-09, REQ-10.

Depends on: [ERA-021](ERA-021.md), [ERA-010](ERA-010.md), [ERA-005](ERA-005.md).

## Intended implementation ownership

- `backend/cases/`
- `frontend/blockers/`
- `backend/source-registry/`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record reason/category, affected scope, responsible actor, timestamps, and supporting reference for blockers.
- [ ] Demonstrate policy hold, missing mapping, stale source, unsupported interval, and unknown outcome.
- [ ] Resolving a blocker regenerates or revalidates affected proposals; it never implicitly authorizes execution.
- [ ] Source deactivation or registration during a request is visible and cannot silently reduce its snapshotted coverage.

## Verification

Walk through hold/release, mapping repair, source deactivation, and unknown-outcome intervention; assert preserved history and fresh review where required.

Retain commands/results and owner review against the exact candidate. Application checks are implemented with the issue; these are acceptance instructions, not claims that tests already exist.

## Boundaries

Use the [product requirements](../../docs/specification.md) and [architecture contract](../../docs/architecture.md). Operate on synthetic data for the demonstration. Changes to an approved scope or operation require new review. External deployment and GitHub publication follow their own owner authorization.
